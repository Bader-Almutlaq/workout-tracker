# JEM: page-by-page direction from the owner (2026-10-31)

The owner reviewed JEM against Strong and gave this direction. Strong screenshots are kept **outside the repository** (they contain personal data, and the repository is public). On the lead's machine they live in `.claude/worktrees/references/strong/`, and the lead attaches them to agents that need them. This document describes what they show, so it stands on its own.

Earlier decisions still hold: `docs/design/PROPOSALS.md` → *Owner decisions*.

## Words

- **Plan:** all the workouts of a week (e.g. "Full 4"). Today's template *folder*.
- **Workout:** one day of a plan (e.g. "Full Bench Sat"). Today's *template*.
- **Session:** one performed workout (a History entry).
- **Body part:** a measured place on the body (neck, left biceps…). Never "site" on screen.

## Navigation

- **Tab bar:** Workout · History · Exercises · Measure · Analytics.
  - Strong's bar (screenshot 01) is Profile, History, Start Workout, Exercises, Measure.
  - **Settings leaves the tab bar.** It becomes a gear button at the top right of each main tab screen.
- **Analytics** (decided by the lead while the owner is away): the overview hub for training-wide analytics.
  - Each thing's own analytics live inside its page (body part, exercise, workout, plan).
  - The Analytics tab drops its Body section, since Measure owns that now.
  - The full analytics redesign comes last, after measurements, exercises and workouts.
- **Pages with sections** use one segmented bar at the top, like Strong's "About · History · Charts · Records" and JEM's existing "Training · Body" bar. The same component everywhere.

## Charts over long periods (applies to every new chart)

The owner's chart review: daily points over months or years can't be read.

- **Aggregate by period:** the small chart picks its bucket from the range shown. Raw points for up to about 3 months, weekly beyond that, monthly beyond about 2 years. It plots the bucket's value (the mean for measurements; the best for strength), with the range as a faint band.
- **Tap the chart to open it full screen,** with a range picker (3M, 6M, 1Y, All) and finer detail.
- Keep JEM's existing chart rules: thin lines, recessive grid, SVG via d3-scale/d3-shape.
- The analytics overhaul later reuses this component.

## Measure tab (screenshots 02–03)

- **List** (screenshot 02):
  - Body parts grouped, latest value on the right, and a **+** button on each row to log quickly.
  - Left/right pairs stay together on one row, as JEM does today (Strong lists them separately; the owner prefers pairs).
  - Body parts never measured are **hidden**. "Not measured yet" is gone; the owner can show a hidden part from "Add body part".
  - **Bodyweight is not listed.** Keep its data; just don't show it here.
- **Adding new body parts:** kept. The owner likes it.
- **Ab definitions** (the default "where to measure" notes):
  - Lower abs: at the hip bone.
  - Waist (middle abs): at the belly button.
  - Upper abs: above the belly button, below the ribs.
- **Body part page** (screenshot 03, Strong's sheet with a chart, Add Measurement and History):
  - A segmented bar: **Chart · History**.
  - **Chart** uses the long-period chart above, with change figures.
  - **History** lists every entry, newest first, editable.
  - The **+** button or **Log** button records a new value (relaxed/flexed where it applies).
  - The "where and how to measure" note stays reachable from this page.

## Exercises (screenshots 04–08)

- **Search stays as it is.** It's better than Strong's.
- **Exercise page:** a segmented bar instead of today's one long page.
  - **Summary:** primary and secondary muscles, equipment, loading settings, and later the quality profile and machine.
  - **History:** every session of this exercise, newest first.
  - **Charts:** all of this exercise's analytics. Strong (screenshots 06–07) shows Best set (est. 1RM), Best set (max weight), Total volume, PR progression (as 1RM) and Max consecutive reps. Keep the useful ones, improved like JEM's analytics, with the long-period chart.
  - **Records:** personal records (1RM, weight, max volume) and the rep-max table with best performance and predicted weight (screenshot 08).
  - **How-to:** the picture and the steps.
- **Pictures:** the owner prefers an illustrated model with highlighted muscles (Strong's style, screenshot 05) over photos of a real person. Nice to have, not required.

## Plans and workouts (screenshots 09–10)

- Strong shows plans as folders of workout cards (09). The owner wants a different look; the lead chooses.
- **Plan page:** tapping a plan opens its page. A segmented bar: **Workouts · History · Analytics**.
  - **Workouts:** cards with name, exercise summary and last done.
  - **History:** sessions of any of its workouts.
  - **Analytics:** that plan's trends.
- **Workout page:** tapping a workout opens a page with a description, last done date, and **Exercises · History · Analytics** tabs. A pinned **Start this workout** button starts it.
  - Exercise rows open History and Charts scoped to sessions started from this workout, with a link to all sessions of the exercise.

## Live workout (screenshots 11–13)

- **Strong's layout:** compact, and the owner prefers it.
  - The header holds workout metadata (date, time).
  - Each exercise has its name, then an **info line** (rest time, machine, notes), then the columns **Set · Previous · kg · Reps · ✓**.
- **Values:**
  - Weight and reps show greyed suggestions from the previous session. Ticking accepts them.
  - An **amber** suggestion sits beside a value and is used only when tapped. Progression and warm-up suggestions use this.
  - RPE: Strong shows it as a small badge on the tick (screenshot 11). JEM may do the same to stay compact.
- **Warm-ups:** above the working sets, in the same layout, tinted a **brighter purple** with a thin line before the first working set.
- **Set types:** tapping the set number classifies the set. W warm-up, D drop, F failure, with **F in red** like Strong.
- **Exercise menu (⋯)**, as in Strong (screenshots 12–13). The owner rarely uses these, but they're good:
  - add note, add sticky note, add warm-up sets
  - rest time (display only; no automatic rest timers)
  - replace exercise, create superset
  - preferences: **unit matters**; bar type is unimportant
  - remove exercise
- **Metrics focus:** Strong lets you pick metrics to watch per exercise (volume, volume increase, reps…). Undecided; leave it for later.
